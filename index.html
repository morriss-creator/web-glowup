FAQ

Straight answers.

What does it cost?

Every site is priced on its own. The range is From $199 up to $1,500. Price by quote. It's a one-time payment. No packages and no monthly fee. The quote is free, and you see the number before any work starts.

How long does it take?

About 7 days from the draft to launch, if notes, photos, and logins show up on time. The free intro call is the start.

What do I need to send you?

A few notes on what you offer, your hours, your address, a logo if you have one, and photos. Phone photos are fine to start. If booking is included, I need access to Google and to your calendar. We'll sort out anything that's missing.

Can I change things later?

Yes. After launch we talk through changes and price them one by one. There is no monthly plan.

Who owns the site?

You do. The words and photos you provide, and the finished site, are yours. You get the files and the logins. The domain and the Google profile stay in your name.
